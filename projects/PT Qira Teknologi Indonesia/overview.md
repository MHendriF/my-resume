# PT Qira Teknologi Indonesia - Project Portfolio & Overview

## 📌 Ringkasan Perusahaan & Peran
* **Perusahaan:** PT Qira Teknologi Indonesia
* **Peran:** Software Developer (Contract / Project-based)
* **Periode:** Nov 2020 – Jan 2024
* **Tipe Sistem:** Enterprise Assessment Platforms, CRM, E-Commerce, Payment Gateways & Companion Mobile Apps

---

## 🎯 Masalah & Solusi
Membangun dan mengelola berbagai platform digital kelas enterprise untuk klien korporat, mencakup modul rekrutmen/asesmen online, e-commerce, CRM, serta integrasi payment gateway otomatis untuk rekonsiliasi transaksi tanpa intervensi manual.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Arsitektur
| Komponen | Teknologi | Keterangan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Backend Framework** | PHP (Laravel), Node.js | RESTful APIs, asynchronous job queue |
| **Database & Cache** | MySQL, Redis | Relational data schema & index optimization |
| **Payment Gateways** | Midtrans (Core/Snap), Moota | VA, QRIS, E-Wallet, bank mutation reconciliation |
| **Mobile Client** | Java, Kotlin, Android Jetpack, Firebase | Companion apps for field staff & customers |
| **Server & Deployment**| Linux/SSH, cPanel, Plesk | Zero-downtime deployment pipelines |

---

## 🚀 Fitur-Fitur Kunci yang Dibangun
1. **Automated Payment Gateway & Mutation Reconciliation:** Webhook integrasi Midtrans & Moota untuk memverifikasi transaksi instan dengan akurasi 99.9%.
2. **Online Assessment & Scoring Engine:** Platform tes psikometri online dengan anti-cheat randomize questions, timer, dan auto-scoring PDF report.
3. **Companion Android Mobile Apps:** Aplikasi mobile untuk sinkronisasi data real-time berbasis Firebase.

---

## 📈 Metrik Pencapaian
* **99.9% Billing Accuracy:** Otomasi 100% verifikasi invoice klien tanpa kesalahan rekonsiliasi manual.
* **High-Concurrency Assessment:** Mampu menampung ribuan peserta ujian bersamaan tanpa bottleneck performa database.
